- **Portrait mode:** Main menu → Screen: landscape, portrait, or portrait flipped. The screen turns at once and stays so after a restart.
- **The play screen upright is a tape deck:** the cassette in the deck's door (the side, A internal or B card, and the album on its label; the place in the queue between the turning reels; the format on its notch), then the deck's display (title, artist, date and time, the jack, elapsed / volume / length, progress, BT, EQ, ReplayGain, a lossy file's bitrate), the level meters, and transport keys that show the state (play, pause or stop pressed).
- **Upright too:** the browser and the main menu (18 rows) and the power-off screen. The equalizer, Bluetooth, buttons, date and time, audiobooks, About, the USB disk, the installer and the charging screen still open in landscape for now.
- **The buttons follow the screen:** upright, the VOL key pointing up moves up through lists and turns the volume up, whichever way round you hold the player.
